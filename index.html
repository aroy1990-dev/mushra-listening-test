Blind listening study

Which one sounds like the player alone?

Someone clicked on one musician in a video, and several methods tried to return only that musician's sound. On each of the 10 pages you see a picture from the video with the clicked musician in a red box, and you can play the reference: that musician recorded alone.

The picture only shows you which musician was clicked. Judge the clips by ear only.

Then rate each of 7 clips from 0 to 100 for how close it sounds to the reference. Mark a clip down for every other musician you can hear, and for distortion or missing notes. One of the clips is the reference itself.

Please wear headphones in a quiet room. Differences are easy to miss on laptop or phone speakers.

All clips are matched for loudness, so please leave your volume alone during the test. The order of the pages and of the clips is shuffled for every listener.
